# Required Onboarding Resources

Every developer must complete this checklist, regardless of experience level
or chosen learning path. These requirements accompany the
[curriculum phases](../README.md#curriculum); they are part of onboarding
completion.

Source: [Cristian's onboarding requirements, October 5, 2026](https://bloclabsworkspace.slack.com/archives/C0C6Z6T6QAW/p1791255850548569).
The resources below link directly to their publishers so this checklist can
be used without access to the Slack message.

## Certification and course requirements

- [ ] Earn the [AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate certification](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-solutions-architect-associate/).
  Prepare alongside [AWS & Cloudflare](../phase-5-bloclabs-stack/06-aws-and-cloudflare.md).
  Record the certification credential as completion evidence.
- [ ] Complete **at least one course** from the
  [Anthropic partner certifications catalog](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications).
  Work through it alongside [Phase 4](../README.md#phase-4-ai-augmented-development).
  Record the course name and its completion evidence. One course is the minimum.

## Must-read books

Read both books. Selected chapters and summaries do not replace completing
the required reading. The [education library](README.md) provides book access.

- [ ] [Designing Data-Intensive Applications](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-data-intensive-applications/9781491903063/)
  by Martin Kleppmann. Pair with [System Design](../phase-6-mastery/01-system-design.md).
- [ ] [AI Engineering](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-engineering/9781098166298/)
  by Chip Huyen. Pair with [Phase 4](../README.md#phase-4-ai-augmented-development).

For each book, be ready to explain its main ideas and how they affect an
engineering decision, using an example from your work or a practice exercise.

## Required Ardan Labs understanding

Understand each of these guides **at a high level**. Be able to describe the
main design choices and how they relate to service architecture; detailed
implementation mastery is not required for this checklist.

- [ ] [Reading](https://github.com/ardanlabs/service/wiki/reading)
- [ ] [Design Guidelines](https://github.com/ardanlabs/service/wiki/design-guidelines)
- [ ] [Domain-Driven, Data-Oriented Design](https://github.com/ardanlabs/service/wiki/Domain-Driven,-Data-Oriented-Design)
- [ ] [Project Structure](https://github.com/ardanlabs/service/wiki/project-structure)
- [ ] [Modules](https://github.com/ardanlabs/service/wiki/modules)

The Reading page also points to material for deeper study later. Its linked
reading list does not add further must-read books to this checklist.

## Completion

Mark an item complete when you have earned the certification, finished the
course or book, or can explain the required reading at the depth stated above.
Keep your certification and course evidence with your onboarding progress.
Onboarding is complete only when all items above and your curriculum path
are complete. If access to a course or book is blocked, report it to your
onboarding contact and leave that item open until resolved.
